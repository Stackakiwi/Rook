How Rook Survival works

1 · The goal

The Wildwood campaign has 12 levels across four forest regions. Defeat the required Hollowborn guardians, survive elite attacks, and beat a Warden at the end of each region. Level 12 ends with the Wildwood Regent.

2 · Rook levels

Enemies drop blue XP motes. Collect enough and Rook levels up. Leveling is deliberately slower now, but every level restores health, awards gold, and gives a saved Upgrade Point. Every fifth Rook level gives an extra milestone reward.

3 · Upgrade Points

Open Inventory at any time. Spend points on Attack, Defense, Health, or Speed—or save several points and decide later. The game shows ranks, not hidden percentages.

4 · Gold & gear

Gold pickups and level rewards are banked when a run ends. Spend banked gold in the Armory to permanently unlock weapons, Guardian Dash, and Core Abilities. Equip your preferred loadout before entering the woods.

5 · Controls

Computer: WASD/arrows move, mouse aims, hold click or Space attacks, I opens Inventory, Shift uses Dash, Q uses the equipped Core Ability, Esc/P pauses.
Phone: left thumbstick moves; ATTACK, DASH, and the Core Ability appear on the right when available.

6 · Pickups

Blue motes give XP. Gold builds your bank. Heart leaves restore health. Pickups pull toward Rook when he gets close, so you do not have to touch every drop exactly.
